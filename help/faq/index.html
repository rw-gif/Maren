FAQ

Answers point to the relevant policy page, so there is only ever one version of a rule.

Orders & Payment

Do your prices include VAT?

Yes — the price you see includes UK VAT and any mandatory charges. Delivery is shown before you pay.

How will I know my order is confirmed?

Your order is an offer; the contract is formed when we send your dispatch confirmation. See our Terms.

Shipping

What are the delivery options and times?

UK standard and express options, free over the threshold, plus international. Full details and windows are on Shipping & Delivery.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to selected regions. Import duties and taxes may apply on arrival — see Duties & Taxes.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your returns policy?

You have a statutory 14-day right to cancel, plus our goodwill window for unworn items with tags. See Returns & Exchanges.

My item is faulty. What now?

Your Consumer Rights Act protections apply in full and are separate from change-of-mind returns. Contact us and we'll put it right.

Sizing & Fit

Where is your size guide?

Measurements and fit notes are on the Size Guide & Care page.

Product & Care (linen/cotton)

How do I care for linen?

Cold machine wash, reshape while damp, dry flat or line dry, avoid tumble drying. A relaxed crease is part of the character of good linen.

Account & Privacy

Do I need an account to order?

No — guest checkout is always available. If you do create an account, we use a passwordless sign-in. See Account.
