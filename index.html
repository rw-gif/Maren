How the production version works

This prototype layers a flat garment graphic over a chosen figure to demonstrate the flow and UI. A live build would route the customer's photo (or a chosen body model) and the garment's 3D/2D asset through a try-on engine — either a specialist virtual try-on API/SDK, or an in-house diffusion/pose-estimation pipeline — returning a photorealistic composite. The garment catalogue, sizing logic and "add to bag" handoff wire into the same product data used by The Edit.

Privacy note: any feature that ingests a customer photo processes personal (and arguably biometric) data. Under UK GDPR you'll need a clear lawful basis, a privacy notice covering it, retention limits, and ideally on-device or ephemeral processing. Worth confirming with counsel before launch.

The faces of the brand

The Muses

Cami and Vivi are the two women MAREN is cut for — the same easy, sunlit life in two registers. Cami carries the considered edit; Vivi carries the everyday.

CAMI

The Curator · Cool & composed

Cool icy-blonde, porcelain-calm and quietly put-together. Cami is MAREN's editorial register — a crisp white linen shirt, tailored neutrals, clean lines and a steady gaze. She carries the considered pieces: buy well, wear them for years.

Portrait — add vivi.jpg

VIVI

The Spark · Golden & sun-kissed

Golden-blonde and sun-kissed, freckles and an easy mid-laugh. Vivi is the everyday heart of MAREN — breton stripes, gingham and washed cotton, barefoot on the deck in the late light. Warm, candid and unfussy, she makes an ordinary morning feel like a holiday.
