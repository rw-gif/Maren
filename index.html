How the production version works

This prototype layers a flat garment graphic over a chosen figure to demonstrate the flow. A live build routes the customer’s photo (or a body model) and the garment asset through a try-on engine — a specialist VTO API or an in-house diffusion/pose pipeline — returning a photorealistic composite, wired to the same product data as The Edit.

Privacy note: any feature ingesting a customer photo processes personal (and arguably biometric) data. Under UK GDPR you’ll need a lawful basis, a privacy notice, retention limits, and ideally on-device or ephemeral processing. Confirm with counsel before launch.
