# PRELAUNCH.md — Maren go-live gate

The site stays password-protected / unpublished until **every box below is ticked.**
Building and previewing in dev before this is fine; publishing is not.

## 1. Placeholders filled
- [ ] Every value in `site-config.json` completed; `_status` changed from `DRAFT` to `READY`.
- [ ] Whole site searched for the character `[` — zero unfilled `[TOKEN]` remain on any page.
- [ ] Business identity (legal name, company number, address, VAT number, email) shows
      correctly on the Contact page and in the footer.

## 2. Legal review (do not skip)
- [ ] Privacy Policy reviewed by a UK-qualified solicitor.
- [ ] Cookie Policy reviewed.
- [ ] Terms of Service + Terms of Sale reviewed — especially contract formation
      (offer/acceptance on dispatch), pricing-error clause, and limitation of liability.
- [ ] Returns & Exchanges reviewed against the statutory minimum (14-day cancellation).

## 3. Data protection / consent
- [ ] ICO data protection fee registered and paid; reg number in the Privacy Policy.
- [ ] Cookie banner blocks non-essential/advertising scripts until consent; Accept all /
      Reject all / Manage preferences equally prominent; footer "Cookie settings" works.
- [ ] Consent is logged (timestamp, categories, policy version).
- [ ] Newsletter sign-up uses unticked positive consent; unsubscribe works; business
      identity present in emails.

## 4. Consumer / pricing
- [ ] All displayed prices include VAT and any mandatory charges (no drip pricing).
- [ ] If product reviews are enabled: only genuine reviews published, and a process exists
      to prevent/remove fakes.
- [ ] Shipping page separates processing time from transit time; windows are estimates.
- [ ] If shipping internationally: duties/taxes position stated clearly.

## 5. Disclosure
- [ ] AI-generated imagery disclosure line live (FAQ + footer/About).
- [ ] Affiliate terms require #ad disclosure per ASA/CAP before any affiliate goes live.

## 6. Baseline
- [ ] Commit tagged as launch-ready baseline once all of the above pass.
