B. Terms of Sale

Contract formation

Your order is an offer. A contract forms only when we send a dispatch confirmation; we may decline orders. This means a mispriced or out-of-stock item does not bind us to sell at the wrong price, because no contract exists until we confirm dispatch.

Pricing

The total price shown is inclusive of VAT and any mandatory charges, up front (no drip pricing). An obvious pricing-error clause lets us cancel and refund genuine errors, provided we act before dispatch.

Payment, title and risk

Your statutory quality rights

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 goods must be of satisfactory quality, as described, and fit for purpose. These rights cannot be excluded; we state them, we do not waive them.

Limitation of liability

Liability is capped where lawful, but we never exclude liability for death or personal injury from negligence, for fraud, or for non-excludable consumer rights.
