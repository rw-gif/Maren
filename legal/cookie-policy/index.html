What cookies are

Small files placed on your device that let a site work, remember preferences, and understand how it is used. We also use similar technologies such as pixels and local storage; we refer to all of these as "cookies".

The categories we use

  • Strictly necessary — required to run the site, your basket and checkout, and to keep it secure. These do not need your consent and cannot be switched off.
  • Performance / analytics — first-party cookies that help us measure how the site performs so we can improve it. You can opt out at any time using "Cookie settings".
  • Functional / preferences — remember choices such as region, currency or language.
  • Advertising — set by us or our partners (for example Meta and TikTok) to measure and tailor advertising. We only use these with your consent.
